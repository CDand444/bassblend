Room builder

htTamer · photos, sizes and notes → your room's shape

1 · Sizes and notes

Feet, as measured. The longest straight distance front to back (the screen wall is the front) and side to side, and the ceiling height at its highest. What you type wins over what the AI sees in photos.

Have a floor plan?

An architect's or estate agent's plan, a sketch with sizes, or a screenshot from a floor plan app. The AI takes the shape, the doors and openings and the sizes written on it. PDFs: take a screenshot of the page first.

Photos of the room still help with the ceiling and openings.

2 · Photos

Stand in a corner and photograph the wall opposite, then the next corner, until every wall is covered. Add the openings and doors, and the ceiling if it slopes. Up to 8 photos; they're shrunk on this phone before sending.

  • Tell the AI which wall each photo shows (the screen wall is the front).
  • A tape measure in the shot helps.

Uses your AI assistant (one request). Nothing is saved until you press Save.

The AI is looking at your room. This can take a minute…

3 · Your room

LiDAR scanning isn't available to web pages on iPhone (only native apps can use it), so this builder works from photos, sizes and notes. Fine-tune the result any time on the dashboard under Room and subs.
