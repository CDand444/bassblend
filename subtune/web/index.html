      Blend the subs with your speakers

      Plays a test through the receiver: first your front speakers on their own, then again with the subs. BassBlend works out whether subs and speakers add up or cancel around the crossover, and how to fix the timing and level. Volume stays where you set it; everything is put back afterwards.
